Architecture diagram

Compact system-of-surfaces view. Rendered 2026-08-08. View of skill-design.md as of this timestamp; Markdown is the current source of truth and this view must be re-rendered after design changes.

Thesis

Compile a semantic graph through seven executable, help-gated CLI leaves. Stable semantic and idempotency identities remain agent-owned; renderer fields remain server-owned.

Prime invariant

Graph meaning and the rendered board must agree. Accepted syntax or stored membership alone cannot prove correct direction, grouping, or hierarchy. Every non-empty semantic group must project to one server-owned, locked, unlabeled enclosure around exactly its members; that enclosure is not a public semantic entity or label target.

Sign-off

Current source and the rebuilt checkout expose all seven scene leaves. A fresh board may have no managed draft: only typed whiteboard.draft-missing permits one first idempotent mutation, whose receipt and immediate successful inspect prove initialization. After publication, session url mints the private review handoff without another event, revision, or session; open is not used as a review-link helper. Group enclosure behavior must still survive strict projection, replay, legacy-upgrade, rendered review, and fresh-agent proof. Registry installation, Claude/Cursor activation, rendered coherence, and human acceptance remain separate proof surfaces.
